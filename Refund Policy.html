Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Final Sale Policy

Due to the digital nature of our assets, all transactions are final once the delivery process is initiated. We do not offer cash refunds under any circumstances.

2. Replacement Guarantee

We provide a 24-hour replacement guarantee only if the product is found to be non-functional (invalid) immediately upon delivery. You must provide clear video evidence or a screenshot from the moment of receipt.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds or replacements will not be issued if:

  • The account or asset was banned or restricted due to user behavior.
  • The user changed the account credentials (email/password).
  • The user claims "accidental purchase."

4. Chargeback Policy

Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute through your bank or payment gateway will result in an immediate permanent ban from DIT Shop and may lead to legal action for fraud.
