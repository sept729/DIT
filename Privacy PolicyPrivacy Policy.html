Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

This page explains what DIT Shop collects when you use the site, why we collect it, and what happens to it afterwards. It's written to be read, not to be skipped. If anything here isn't clear, ask us on Discord and we'll explain it.

Who to contact

DIT Shop is an independent online shop. The person responsible for your information can be reached here:

Email: ihabazzouz140@gmail.com

Discord: our support server — usually the fastest way to reach us

What we collect

Only what we need to take your order and deliver it:

  • Your email address — creates your account and signs you in.
  • Your name — optional. Only stored if you type it when signing up, or if you sign in with Google.
  • Your Discord username — how we deliver your order and answer your questions.
  • Your order details — what you bought, the total, the payment method you picked, and whether the order is still waiting or delivered.

We do not collect or store card numbers, bank details, or crypto wallet keys. Payment happens entirely outside this site — on PayPal, or directly on the blockchain. Nothing about your payment method ever passes through our servers.

Who handles it for us

The site runs on Google Firebase, which manages sign-in and stores your account and orders on Google's servers. Google's own privacy terms cover that processing — you can read them at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

If you sign in with Google, Google passes us your email address and display name. We get nothing else from your Google account.

Delivery and support happen on Discord, so anything you send us in a ticket is also covered by Discord's own terms.

Those two are the only companies involved. We don't work with advertisers, analytics providers, or data brokers.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone outside the two services above.
  • We don't send marketing emails.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The only things stored in your browser are your sign-in session and your shopping cart, so they survive a page refresh.

How we protect it

Access to the database is restricted by server-side rules, not by anything in your browser. You can only read your own orders, and only we can change them once they're placed. The connection to the site is encrypted end to end.

No system is perfect. If we ever discover that your information has been exposed, we'll tell you directly rather than quietly fixing it.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept for 12 months so we can honour warranties and handle replacements. After that they're deleted. Your account stays for as long as you want it.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Message us on Discord or email us and we'll deal with it within 30 days, usually much sooner.

One thing worth knowing before you ask: deleting your account also deletes your order history, and without that history we can't honour replacements on orders you've already placed.

You can clear your cart and sign-in session yourself at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Age

You need to be 18 or older to buy from this shop, because every payment method we accept requires it. If you believe someone underage has created an account, contact us and we'll remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. We won't make a change quietly and hope nobody notices.
